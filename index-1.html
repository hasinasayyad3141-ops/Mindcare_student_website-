About Problem Mood Tracker Tips

Community Introduction

Students face academic pressure, deadlines, exams, friendship issues and uncertainty about the future. MindCare is a simple student-friendly website that encourages students to notice their feelings and take a small positive step.

Problem Statement

Many students experience stress, sadness, anxiety or anger but may not know how to express their feelings or where to start. MindCare provides an easy and supportive first step through mood tracking and positive guidance.

💡 Small Things That Can Help

🫁 Take a pause

Take a few slow breaths and give yourself a short break.

📚 Study smart

Break large tasks into smaller, manageable goals.

💬 Talk to someone

Share your feelings with a trusted friend, teacher or family member.

🌱 Be kind to yourself

A difficult day does not define your ability or your future.
